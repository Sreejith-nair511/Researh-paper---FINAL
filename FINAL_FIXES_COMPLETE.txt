FIGURE PLACEMENT RATIONALE
================================================================================

[h] Placement:
- H = "Here" - tries to place figure at current position first
- If doesn't fit on current page, moves to next page
- Prevents excessive floating and displacement
- Keeps figures near related text
- More predictable positioning

\linewidth Sizing:
- Automatically scales to single column width
- Maintains aspect ratio of images
- Fills column nicely without overflow
- Works across all IEEE paper formats
