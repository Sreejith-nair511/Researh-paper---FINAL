TECHNICAL DETAILS

FIGURE PLACEMENT OPTIONS EXPLAINED:
- [H] : Forces placement HERE (can break two-column layout) - REMOVED
- [t] : Preferred at top of page (IEEE standard) - APPLIED
- [b] : At bottom of page
- [p] : On dedicated float page

WHY [t] IS BETTER:
- Allows LaTeX to optimize page breaks
- Maintains proper two-column flow
- Follows IEEE conference paper standards
- Reduces visual artifacts and gaps

ABOUT CAPTIONS:
- LaTeX-generated captions: Professional and consistent
- Embedded captions: Hard to update, causes layout issues
- All captions now properly managed by LaTeX
